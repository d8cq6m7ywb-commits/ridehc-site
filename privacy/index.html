ridehc / Privacy

Privacy policy

Applies to RideHC for iPhone, version 1.0. Last updated 27 October 2026.

The short version

RideHC keeps everything on your phone. It has no account of its own, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing you record leaves the phone unless you export or share a file yourself, or, if you connect Grepiac, tap Send to Grepiac on a finished ride. Connecting Grepiac, sending a ride and disconnecting are the only things in the app that use the internet.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your rider profile: the name you enter, FTP, weight, threshold and maximum heart rate, resting heart rate, birth date, and your training-zone settings. Each is optional.
  • Your rides: every value your sensors send during a session — power, cadence, heart rate, speed, core and skin temperature, muscle oxygen and total haemoglobin, battery levels — with the sensor it came from, its units and quality flags; the workout you rode; the targets the app set; and a raw log of the Bluetooth traffic, kept for diagnosing sensor problems.
  • Your sensors: the Bluetooth identifiers, names and nicknames of the devices you have connected, so the app finds them again.
  • Your workouts: the workout library, including files you import.

All of it is stored in the app's own storage on your iPhone, protected by the device's encryption and your passcode. The app does not use HealthKit and does not read or write Apple Health.

Heart rate and other health-related data

Heart rate, body temperature and muscle oxygen are health-related measurements. RideHC records them only to show them to you during the ride and to write them into your ride files. They are never used for advertising, and they leave the phone only in a file you share or a ride you send to Grepiac yourself.

Bluetooth

RideHC asks for Bluetooth so it can read your sensors and set the resistance on a smart trainer. It keeps Bluetooth running in the background so a ride keeps recording with the screen off. It does not use Bluetooth for anything else.

Files you export or share

You can export a ride as a FIT file, a CSV file and a JSON archive, and share them with the system share sheet, AirDrop or Files. Those files contain the ride's data and the profile values the ride used, such as your name, FTP and weight. Once a file has left the phone it is governed by whatever service you sent it to, not by RideHC.

Sending a ride to Grepiac (optional)

Grepiac is a coaching platform run by the same developer as RideHC. If you have a Grepiac account, you can connect it in RideHC's Settings: you sign in on grepiac.com in a secure Apple sign-in sheet, and Grepiac asks you to approve one permission, to upload activities. RideHC never sees your Grepiac password, name or email; it keeps only the sign-in token Grepiac gives it, in the iPhone's Keychain on this device.

Nothing is sent when you connect. A ride goes to Grepiac only when you tap Send to Grepiac on its summary, one ride per tap; nothing is sent in the background and nothing is retried on its own. What is sent is that ride's FIT file: the ride's measurements (power, cadence, speed, distance, heart rate, core and skin temperature, muscle oxygen and total haemoglobin), its laps and the targets the app set; the names, serial numbers and firmware versions of the sensors that recorded it; and a number RideHC makes up for this installation of the app, which is not your iPhone's identifier. The file does not contain your weight, FTP, birth date or name. It is, however, named after the ride's date and the rider name in your profile (for example 20261027T165152Z-rider.fit), and Grepiac keeps that file name with the ride; the app's version number goes with the upload too. On Grepiac the ride is added to your activities, where you and your coach can see it, and it can mark the matching planned session completed. Grepiac's handling of it is described in Grepiac's privacy notice.

You can disconnect at any time with Disconnect in RideHC's Settings, or by removing RideHC under Settings → Connected apps on grepiac.com. Rides you have already sent stay on Grepiac until you delete them there.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your iPhone deletes everything it stored. Exported files you have saved elsewhere are not affected, and neither are rides you sent to Grepiac. If you connected Grepiac, disconnect first, or remove RideHC under Connected apps on grepiac.com afterwards; otherwise the link expires on its own within 30 days. A new installation of RideHC never reuses an old sign-in.

Children

RideHC is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If a future version changes what the app does with your data, this page will say so before that version is released, with the date of the change. 26 October 2026: added the optional link to Grepiac. 27 October 2026: said that the sent file is named after your rider name, and that it carries total haemoglobin.
