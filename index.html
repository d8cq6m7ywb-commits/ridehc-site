ridehc
Hors Catégorie

An open cycling and studio training app for iPhone.

RideHC records every sensor you connect — trainer, power meter, heart rate, cadence, speed, core temperature, muscle oxygen — runs structured workouts in ERG or by target, and writes standard FIT and CSV files you can take anywhere. It works on a smart trainer, on rollers, and on a studio bike at the gym.

What it stands for

  • Your data is yours. Rides live on your phone. Export is free in every version, permanently, and nothing is uploaded unless you share a file yourself.
  • Record everything, display some of it. Every connected stream is stored with its source, units and quality flags, even when there is no screen for it yet.
  • Honest numbers. A sensor dropout is a gap in the file, never a stale value filled in.
  • No account, no cloud, no tracking. The app has no network code.

Where it is

RideHC 1.0 is being prepared for the App Store. It needs an iPhone running iOS 17 or later and Bluetooth sensors: a smart trainer or power meter, and a heart-rate strap or a watch that broadcasts heart rate.

Group sessions for coaches and clubs, and an optional link to a coaching platform, come in later versions.
